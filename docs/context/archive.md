An archive page in a blog is a structured listing page that displays multiple blog posts, typically organized chronologically.

**Archive Page Structure:**

An archive page serves as an index or catalog of blog content. It contains:

**Layout Pattern:**
- A collection of post previews/cards arranged vertically or in a grid
- Each post entry typically includes:
  - Post title (usually linked to the full post)
  - Publication date
  - Excerpt or summary (first few sentences or a custom description)
  - Author name (optional)
  - Category/tags (optional)
  - Featured image/thumbnail (optional)
  - "Read more" link or button

**Organization Methods:**
- **Chronological**: All posts listed by date (newest first, typically)
- **By Month/Year**: Posts grouped under date headers like "December 2024", "November 2024"
- **By Category**: Posts grouped by topic/category
- **By Tag**: Posts sharing specific tags
- **Paginated**: Split across multiple pages (e.g., showing 10 posts per page)

**Common Features:**
- Navigation between pages (Previous/Next or page numbers)
- Search or filter functionality
- Sidebar with categories, recent posts, or tags
- Minimal styling compared to individual post pages (focuses on scanning/browsing)

**Purpose:**
Helps users browse all available content, discover older posts, and navigate the blog's full content history rather than just seeing the most recent posts on the homepage.
